1 · Identity — one code per person

There is no SMS rail anywhere in SAAHAA, by design. One permanent code does two jobs.

CodeWhoWhat it does
C20262001customersigns her in and is the door code she reads to the pro
P20262001prosigns him in; printed on his public page
S20262001shopsigns the shop in
Why it is safe to say out loud. The code is a name, not a secret — the password is the secret. It proves the pro was standing at her door because only she could tell him, in person, at the door. One mobile number may hold a customer account and a pro account, each with its own password and wallet.

2 · Signing up — three doors

customer — one step

name + 10-digit mobile + password (8+ chars, PBKDF2 · 250,000 iterations) └─ pick your area └─ C2026xxxx issued, shown once in a dialog with a Copy button └─ live. No approval, no wait.

pro — setup, then 7 verification steps

trade · your rate · area · password └─ 1 phone 2 identity (Aadhaar/PAN — stored as SHA-256 + last 4 only) 3 selfie 4 trade quiz (5 questions, 3 tries, 24h lock after 3 wrong) 5 conduct quiz (6 questions, cannot be failed — it teaches) 6 UPI id 7 agreement ──▶ live immediately. Nobody approves you.
Disclosed at step 7, before you agree: a ₹100 stake locks per job, 10% of each payout waits 7 days, and cancelling a started job costs ₹40. The ₹1,500 cap on your first 3 jobs currently appears only on the "you are live" screen, after signing — a known gap, logged.

shop — name it, photograph it, list stock

shop name · category · FSSAI/drug licence (required for 5 of 8 categories) └─ shopfront photo · place on the map └─ S2026xxxx issued └─ stock from a ready list — about six seconds an item

Licence-gated categories: kirana, fruit & veg, meat, dairy (FSSAI) and pharmacy (drug licence). Water & gas, stationery and pet supplies are not.

3 · Signing in

mobile number OR SAAHAA ID + password ├─ unknown id → "No account with that ID" ├─ wrong password → "Wrong password" └─ correct → straight in. No OTP is ever sent.

A number holding two accounts is asked which one. Language (English / हिन्दी / తెలుగు) survives sign-out.

4 · A service job, end to end

SHE HE ────────────────────────────────── ───────────────────────────── browse category → pick the exact job (no total is shown until she picks — "there is no honest total until you have said which one") choose a pro → THE BILL his price + SAAHAA's 8% on top address (required) → Confirm │ └──── pays by UPI (§7) ──────────▶ he sees the claim, confirms it │ accepts → travelling → arrived │ reads him her C-code at the door ──▶ types it. Wrong code is refused. │ work → photo (or an honest note) │ Confirm & release ◀────────────── "work finished" │ └─ escrow releases: his quote in full, minus a 10% holdback that reaches him after 7 days rate 1–5 ★

5 · Asking several pros — sealed bids

"Ask N pros for their price" (offered only where it can actually save her money) └─ request opens for 12 minutes └─ each pro sees the job + a fair-price band, and sends ONE rate · he can never bid above his own advertised price for that job · he sees no other pro's number └─ window closes → she picks any of them, or keeps her held price └─ accepting creates the order at that rate
Accepting is the last thing that happens: the request is not closed, and rival bids are not rejected, until a job actually exists. A booking that fails leaves the ask answerable.

6 · A shop order, end to end

basket → per-item substitution choice (Similar brand OK / Ask me / Just refund — no reply in 90s = refund, never a silent swap) └─ checkout → pays by UPI (§7) └─ shop accepts → picks & WEIGHS ├─ weighed LIGHT → her bill comes down, difference refunded └─ weighed HEAVY → "she is not charged the extra", shop is told the cost └─ hands to rider (or delivers itself) → she reads her C-code at the door └─ she confirms → settles. Or returns items (§10).

7 · Payment — the manual UPI rail

Until volume pays for an aggregator licence, the rail is one UPI id and two humans.

SHE THE PRO / SHOP THE ADMIN ──────────────────────── ────────────────── ───────────────────── pays saahaa@ptyes exact amount + reference SAxxxxxx (a upi:// link fills all three in) │ types the 12-digit UTR her bank gave her │ ▼ ┌ CLAIMED ┐ ───────────▶ sees it on her phone └─────────┘ confirms │ ▼ ┌ PRO_CHECKED ┐ ─────────▶ matches the UTR in the └─────────────┘ bank statement job may START │ — no money has moved ▼ ┌ CLEARED ┐ └─────────┘ escrow funded. Pro payable next morning.
RuleWhy
A UTR must be exactly 12 digitsthat is what NPCI issues; nothing else is a UTR
One UTR, one live claimotherwise: pay once, claim two jobs. Enforced by a unique index in Postgres, not a check that two requests can both pass
A claim posts no ledger entrya claim that could write to the ledger is a way to mint money by typing
A short payment is not a paid orderescrow is funded with what the statement showed, and the shortfall is recorded
Clearing twice funds oncetired humans double-click at 7am
The risk this accepts, stated plainly. A pro starts work on a claim and is settled next morning on a fact. The gap is bounded to one job and is the reason the payout batch runs after the statement is read. There is no automated defence against a forged UTR — only two people. That is the real cost of running without a payment-aggregator licence.

What is not a charge

The ₹100 stake (or 5% of the job, capped ₹500) and the 10% holdback both come back. Only the ₹40 cancellation fee is money that leaves for good — and the app says so in those words.

Withdrawing

Minimum ₹10, to a UPI id, in full or in part. The maximum offered is always the maximum accepted — balances print to the paisa precisely so the number on the button is a number the validator will take.

Returns are per item, not whole-basket. The shop is told the exact amount before it accepts, and the commission is recomputed on what she actually kept. Complaints freeze the order on both sides and are confirmed before filing — one tap cannot file one by accident.

11 · The admin — role and daily work

Reach it at /#/admin. Separate password, PBKDF2-hashed; the plaintext exists nowhere in the repo.

What only the admin can do

PowerWhy it is not delegated
Clear a paymentthe one action that funds escrow. A customer cannot clear her own; nor can the pro who is owed it
Set the pricing dialsmarkup %, caps, floors — live, without a deploy
Resolve a disputerelease, refund, or split, after reading both sides
Fresh startwipes people and money, keeps the credential and the dials
Read the audit logevery decision, who made it, when

The morning routine

1. open /#/admin → "Payments to clear" (it is the first section, because a pro is waiting) 2. for each row — UTR · amount · reference — search the bank statement: found, right amount → Clear escrow funds, pro becomes payable found, less than bill → Clear with the amount actually seen the shortfall is recorded; chase it not found → Reject she can fix a typo and claim again 3. pay each due pro from the same account 4. record YOUR transfer's UTR against the payout └─ every rupee in has a UTR, every rupee out has a UTR, both on the ledger

What to watch in the first fortnight

  • Claims with no matching statement line. One is a typo. Three from one person is a pattern.
  • The gap between pro-check and clear. Past a morning, the pro is carrying your risk.
  • Anyone paying a different UPI id. The screen names exactly one and says nobody will ever ask for another.

12 · The ledger

Double-entry, hash-chained, append-only — in Postgres a trigger raises on UPDATE or DELETE, because an append-only table that merely promises to be append-only is a comment.

CUSTOMER ──ESCROW_IN──▶ ESCROW:ord ──┬──ESCROW_RELEASE──▶ PARTNER ──HOLDBACK──▶ HOLDBACK ├──FEE─────────────▶ PLATFORM:fee ├──GST─────────────▶ PLATFORM:gst └──REFUND──────────▶ CUSTOMER every order: ESCROW_IN = release + fee + gst ( + rider + dispatch on retail )

Money is stored as integer paise, never a float. Every partner sees his own passbook, to the paisa, with a line that states what the column adds up to and names each pocket it is split across.
